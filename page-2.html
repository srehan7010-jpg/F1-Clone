F1 Circuit Map:

The Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps

This is about 7.004 km (4.352 mi), 19-corner grand prix track located in the ardennes region of Wallonia, Belgium. Key Map (longest track on the current F1 calendar).Number of Corners: 19 turns.Elevation Change: 102.2 meters between its lowest and highest points.Race Laps: 44 laps totaling 308.052 km. Length and Turns: The 19-turn track features massive elevation changes spanning a 102.2-meter difference between its highest and lowest points. La Source: The opening tight right-hand hairpin turn located right after the start/finish line. Eau Rouge and Raidillon: The legendary downhill sweep followed by a steep, blind uphill left-right-left kink where drivers experience intense compression forces. Kemmel Straight: A long, uphill straightaway following Eau Rouge that serves as the track's primary overtaking and DRS (Drag Reduction System) zone leading into Les Combes. Pouhon: A fast, treacherous double-left corner complex that tests high-speed aerodynamic grip.Blanchimont and Chicane: A blistering high-speed left kink taken nearly flat-out, leading down to the final hard-braking chicane before the main straight.
