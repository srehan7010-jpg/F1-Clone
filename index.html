Lewis Carl Davidson Hamilton

Hamilton is a British Formula One racing driver who holds a joint record seven Formula One World Drivers Championship and the all-time record for 106 race wins, Hamilton ending his 15-year association with Mclaren.
He became the first Black driver in F1 and won his first title in 2008.

Ayrton senna da silva

Senna was a legendary Brazilian Formula 1 driver and a three-time world champion widely regarded as one of the greatest and most passionate racers in motosport history.
